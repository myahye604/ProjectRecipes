Description

Classic lasagna is one of Italy's most beloved comfort dishes — a hearty baked pasta layered with rich Bolognese meat sauce, creamy béchamel, and generous amounts of melted cheese. It's the kind of recipe that fills the kitchen with irresistible aromas and brings everyone to the table. This recipe takes a traditional approach, building flavor at every layer. While it takes a little time and patience, the result is a deeply satisfying dish that tastes even better the next day — making it perfect for feeding a crowd or preparing ahead of time.


Ingredients

  • 12 lasagna noodles (or enough to fill your baking dish)
  • 500g (1 lb) ground beef or a mix of beef and pork
  • 1 medium onion, finely diced
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 800g (28 oz) crushed tomatoes
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 1 tsp dried basil
  • Salt and black pepper, to taste
  • 4 tbsp unsalted butter
  • 4 tbsp all-purpose flour
  • 500ml (2 cups) whole milk
  • Pinch of nutmeg
  • 250g (2 cups) shredded mozzarella cheese
  • 100g (1 cup) grated Parmesan cheese
  • Olive oil, for cooking

Steps

  1. Cook the noodles. Boil the lasagna noodles in a large pot of salted water until just al dente. Drain, drizzle lightly with olive oil to prevent sticking, and set aside.
  2. Make the meat sauce. Heat olive oil in a large skillet over medium heat. Sauté the onion until softened, then add the garlic and cook for 1 minute. Add the ground meat and cook until browned, breaking it up as it cooks. Stir in the tomato paste, crushed tomatoes, oregano, and basil. Season with salt and pepper. Simmer on low heat for 20–25 minutes until thickened.
  3. Make the béchamel. In a saucepan, melt the butter over medium heat. Whisk in the flour and cook for 1–2 minutes. Gradually pour in the milk, whisking constantly until smooth and thick. Season with salt, pepper, and a pinch of nutmeg. Remove from heat.
  4. Preheat the oven to 190°C (375°F).
  5. Assemble the lasagna. Spread a thin layer of meat sauce on the bottom of a 9×13-inch baking dish. Add a layer of noodles, then meat sauce, then béchamel, then a sprinkle of mozzarella and Parmesan. Repeat the layers (noodles → meat sauce → béchamel → cheese) until you run out of ingredients, finishing with a generous topping of béchamel and both cheeses.
  6. Bake. Cover the dish with foil and bake for 25 minutes. Remove the foil and bake for another 20–25 minutes, until the top is golden and bubbling.
  7. Rest and serve. Let the lasagna rest for at least 15 minutes before slicing. This helps the layers set so it cuts cleanly. Serve with a simple green salad and crusty bread.
